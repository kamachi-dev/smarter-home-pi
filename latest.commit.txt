fix(hardware-sync): standardize active-high relay polarity, isolate GPIO targeting, and prevent ghost toggles

- Standardized relay switching polarity to Active-High (power ON -> GPIO HIGH writePin(1), power OFF -> GPIO LOW writePin(0)) across RelaySensor, lightingSyncHandler, acSyncHandler, and sensorSyncHandler.
- Removed telemetry-driven lights synchronization loop in SmarterHomeSync to prevent circular ghost-toggling.
- Enhanced LightingSyncHandler, SensorSyncHandler, and ACSyncHandler to strictly match targeted GPIO pins and prevent cross-pin activation.
- Enhanced TapoClient and TemperatureSyncHandler error handling and simulation fallbacks.
- Updated Vitest unit tests for relaySensor, lightingSyncHandler, and sensorSyncHandler.
